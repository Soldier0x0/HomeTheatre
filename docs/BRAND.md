# Home Theater | logo

The brand mark is a manually drawn, two-tone vintage CRT television with curved glass, physical
side controls, and a tripod stand. It is paired with HOME above THEATER, centered and set in the
same font, size, weight, and color. It follows the supplied retro television reference without
copying its rough stroke. The lockup has no background, circle, badge, or tagline.

The app uses [the theme-aware SVG master](../design/prototype/art/brand/home-theater-lockup.svg).
These high-resolution PNG exports have transparent backgrounds and are ready for previews or
platform-specific artwork:

The HOME and THEATER lines are identical in font, size, weight, spacing, and color, and share one
center axis. The television frame uses a muted theme accent; its glass and both text lines use the
theme's light primary tone. Keep the proportions and clear space intact. Do not place the mark inside
a circle or add a glow, bevel, gradient, or extra slogan.
